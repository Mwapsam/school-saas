1.1 Fee Groups (Categories)

Term fees, transport, boarding — the top-level groups students are billed under.

1.2 Fee Masters

Reusable templates for particulars and discounts — define once, link to many categories.

1.3 Fee Particulars

Line items inside each group: tuition, levies, library fee — with amounts and due dates.

1.4 Fine Slabs

Late-payment fines linked to particulars: grace period, slabs, per-day rates.

1.5 Applicability Rules

Control which students fees apply to: all students, by batch, by category, or individually.

1.6 Assign to Course / Batch

Attach fee groups to batches so collections can generate student obligations.
